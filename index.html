01 · About

“Engineer who also has a soul.”

Today I work full-time as an Instrumentation & SCADA Engineer at LabProjects, serving Peruvian water utilities: I maintain the SCADA systems they use to monitor their reservoirs, harden the servers that host them, and calibrate bulk water meters in the field.

I'm just as comfortable debugging a circuit board as I am polishing a user interface. My work lives where mechanics, electronics, and intelligent systems collide — and where careful engineering meets a bit of curiosity.

I'm passionate about creating, exploring, and moving — whether it's through engineering challenges, road trips, fast cars, or quiet moments by the ocean.

03 · Skills

My featured skills

Four places where I like to get my hands dirty — from calibrating flowmeters in the field to breaking systems on purpose.

  1. 01

    Instrumentation & SCADA

    Electromagnetic and ultrasonic flowmeters calibrated in the field, plus the SCADA/HMI (FUXA) and Siemens S7-1200 PLCs that turn their signals into decisions.

  2. 02

    Embedded Systems & Automation

    Microcontrollers, sensor integration, and control algorithms that bridge clean code and the messy physical world.

  3. 03

    Machine Learning & AI

    Training and deploying ML models — from feature engineering on raw sensor data to offline edge inference, like the ensemble behind HydraDetect-AI.

  4. 04

    Ethical Hacking & Pentesting

    Breaking things on purpose to make them safer — from web apps to embedded targets — and hardening the servers that keep SCADA systems online.
