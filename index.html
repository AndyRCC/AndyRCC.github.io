about

Engineer who also has a soul.

I'm just as comfortable debugging a circuit board as I am polishing a user interface. My work lives where mechanics, electronics, and intelligent systems collide — and where careful engineering meets a bit of curiosity.

Right now I'm an Instrumentation Engineer at LabProjects in Ica, validating flow measurement across EMAPICA's wells and reservoirs: I benchmark installed meters against clamp-on ultrasonic flowmeters and tune their correction factors. Before that came EEG/EMG research at UPC, freelance embedded and IoT builds, and bilingual support for U.S. companies.

I'm passionate about creating, exploring, and moving — whether it's through engineering challenges, road trips, fast cars, or quiet moments by the ocean.
